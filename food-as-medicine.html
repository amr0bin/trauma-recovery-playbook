Daily Routine

A fixed strict-keto day, structured to repeat. Protein and fat form the base of every meal; carbohydrate stays low and comes from vegetables and nuts. Breakfast is taken in two parts and held constant; lunch and dinner keep the same shape, with the protein and sauce rotated.

Daily Guidelines
🥩
Protein at every meal — game meats, fish, poultry, and eggs
🥬
Vegetables are the carb budget — lead with leafy greens (~1–2g net/cup); portion cruciferous and coloured veg (~4–6g/cup) to keep the day under 20g net
🎯
Carbohydrate kept low — from vegetables and nuts, not grains, rice, potatoes, or sugar
🥑
Fats — coconut oil, olive oil, avocado, and goat's or sheep's butter
🫙
Fermented vegetables daily — usually with breakfast
🍵
Drinks — tea through the day, water, and bone broth; the occasional coffee
🚫
Avoid — grains, rice, potatoes, sugar, legumes, and starchy roots; most fruit, small amounts of berries aside
🧀
Goat's & sheep's dairy — cheese, yoghurt, butter, and cream; no cow's-milk dairy
Daily Structure
5:00 AM
Breakfast, part one — on waking, before exercise: 3 eggs or a tin of sardines, with avocado and salt. Tea. Walk for early light, then morning workout.
6:30 AM
Breakfast, part two — game-meat patties (venison, bison, kangaroo, or ostrich), smoked salmon with eggs, or sardines. 2 cups greens sautéed in coconut oil plus 1 cup mushrooms or other low-carb veg (~3–4g net). Fermented vegetables. To drink: bone broth with salt and lemon, water, and tea.
11:30 AM
Lunch — chicken, tuna, or salmon over 2 cups Emerald Kale Salad and 1 cup Supergreen Tabbouleh (~7–9g net). A keto sauce, or olive oil and lemon. Walking meditation before.
2:00 PM
Dinner — a grilled, baked, or steamed protein with 2 cups courgette or leafy greens and 1 cup cruciferous (~4–6g net), in butter or my default Green Onion & Ginger Oil. Vigorous exercise after on weekdays.
8:00 PM
Evening — slow walk, bath, meditation, reading, rest.

The Salad Feast Technique

Every lunch and dinner is built as a Salad Feast — a five-part template that turns any combination of seasonal ingredients into a satisfying, complete meal.

ComponentWhat it doesExamples
1. Leading vegThe foundation — prepared to make it specialShaved raw · Roasted · Steamed · Griddled
2. Filling baseThe hearty, satisfying elementCourgette noodles · Broccoli rice · Tabbouleh · Kale salad
3. ContrastLifts and counterpoints the other flavoursQuick pickle · Radicchio · Feta · Olives · Lemon
4. CrunchThe feast feel — never skip thisToasted seeds · Crushed nuts · Dukkah · Whole spices
5. DressingTies everything togetherEVOO + lemon · Salsa verde · Tahini-lemon · Ginger Oil
📊
Targets for every Salad Feast: generous protein · leafy greens as the base (~1–2g net/cup) · cruciferous and coloured veg in smaller portions (~4–6g/cup) · under 20g net carbs across the day.
Emerald Kale Salad — Seasonal Variations

Base: massage 250g shredded kale with 1 tbsp acid + 1 tsp olive oil until silky. Toppings shift with the season. Roughly ~2–2.5g net carbs per cup — the lighter of the two salads, good for volume.

Where the carbs are — and aren't

Protein and fat carry the energy of the day and cost almost nothing against the budget, so I eat them to sufficiency. The 20g is spent almost entirely on vegetables — which is why the vegetables are the only thing I portion by number, and the protein and fat I do not.

🍖
Near-zero — eat to appetite: game, meat, poultry, fish, sardines, smoked salmon, eggs; coconut, olive, and avocado oil; goat's or sheep's butter and ghee; bone broth. Avocado, olives, and goat/sheep/buffalo cheese add only a few grams.
🔺
To eat more, add fat and protein — more oil, more avocado, a fattier cut, an extra patty — at no carb cost. The vegetable portions set the ceiling; the rest of the plate scales up freely.

A full day at ~19g net

Built to eat to sufficiency, with both lunch salads kept in. Only the vegetable portions are capped — protein and fat scale up to appetite.

~3g
Breakfast, part one — 3 eggs in goat's butter + ½ avocado, salt. Tea.
~4g
Breakfast, part two — a large game patty (or smoked salmon and eggs), 2 cups spinach sautéed in coconut oil, 1 cup mushrooms, fermented veg, bone broth.
~9g
Lunch — a generous serving of chicken, tuna, or salmon; 2 cups Emerald Kale Salad + 1 cup Supergreen Tabbouleh; olive oil and lemon.
~3g
Dinner — a grilled or baked protein; 2 cups courgette in butter or Ginger Oil; a leafy side.
📊
Total ≈ 19g net carbs, comfortably under 20g — at whatever calorie level I need, since the protein and fat aren't counted against the budget. This is near the ceiling with both salads in; the lightest levers if a day runs high are less red onion and lemon in the tabbouleh, and leaning on the kale salad for volume.

🍓 Dessert Recipes

Low-carb only. The date-, honey-, and fruit-sweetened desserts have been removed. Below: the keto-friendly fruits worth eating by season, the dark chocolate to reach for, and the one dessert recipe that stays.

Keto Fruit by Season

Fruit is where sugar hides, so I keep to berries and a few low-sugar options, in small portions — a handful, not a bowl. Berries are the most keto-friendly fruit; blueberries are the highest-carb of them, so treat those more sparingly.

🌱 Spring
Early strawberries · rhubarb (tart, usually cooked) · forced rhubarb
☀️ Summer
Raspberries · blackberries · strawberries · redcurrants · gooseberries · blueberries (small amounts)
🍂 Autumn
Blackberries · late raspberries · fresh cranberries (tart) · rosehips · sloes
❄️ Winter
Cranberries · lemons & limes (zest and juice) · a few pomegranate seeds (higher-carb — sparingly)

Dark Chocolate — What's Allowed

Dark chocolate fits keto if you choose a high-cocoa, low-sugar bar and keep to a couple of squares. The higher the cocoa percentage, the less room there is for sugar.

🍫
What to reach for: 85% cocoa or higher (90%+ is ideal). On the label, sugar should sit near the bottom of the ingredient list. Sugar-free bars sweetened with erythritol or stevia are near-zero carb, and 100% cacao or cacao nibs are essentially carb-free — bitter, but excellent shaved over cream or into the cacao pancakes.
🚫
What to skip: milk chocolate and anything under 70% — the carbs climb fast. Keep the portion to 2–3 small squares (about 20g).
