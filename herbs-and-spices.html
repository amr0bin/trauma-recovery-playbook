Crossover flavours. Dill, cilantro, white pepper, cardamom and sesame turn up in more than one region — they're listed where each is most at home, so they're good bridges when you're mixing cuisines on one plate. ✦ marks the starting examples: juniper, za'atar and kombu.

🍵 Tea & Broth Bases

Steep a dried herb like tea, strain, and use it in place of water — or to stretch bone broth
💡
How to use. Steep in just-boiled water for about 15 minutes, strain, then poach chicken or simmer vegetables in it. Season at the end — the tea carries the aroma, salt and fat carry the flavour.
