Reference · relational boundaries

Circles of intimacy

A working map of who sits where, what I share at each distance, and what I can hold. Select any ring or ladder step to cross-reference the two.

Honesty and disclosure are separate Honesty is not saying false things. Disclosure is how many true things I say. A correctly sized answer is not a lie.
Depth is earned, not granted Closeness is built through reciprocal disclosure over time. Sharing past someone's level does not create a container; it removes one.
Placement follows behaviour A person's wish for closeness is not a claim on a circle. What they have done, repeatedly, sets the position.
The circles

Centre outward. Each ring names who belongs there, the setting I see them in, my disclosure ceiling, and the boundary available to me.

Centre is me. Off-map sits outside the system entirely.

Circles reaching this level

Circle 5 is the exception: the ceiling is not fixed. I go one level above whatever they last offered, and only after they have offered.

Movement between circles
Inward — slow, on evidence
  • They reciprocate disclosure at a similar depth.
  • They hold what I gave them without repeating or using it.
  • They behave well when it costs them something — when I am inconvenient, when I say no, when I am not doing well.
  • The third is the test. Many people are warm to a version of me that asks for nothing.
Outward — fast, no hearing required
  • One breach of confidence moves a person out.
  • Repeated pressure past a stated no moves a person out.
  • I owe no notice, explanation, or final conversation.
  • Demotion is a placement decision, not a verdict on their character.
The intermittent pattern

Someone hostile three quarters of the time and warm the rest is not ambivalent. This is an intermittent pattern, and it holds attention more firmly than consistent unkindness because the unpredictable warmth keeps me reassessing.

Placement: Circle 4 at most. Defined context only. Ceiling level 3.

Method: fix the placement for a set period and stop recalculating per interaction. Genuine change would be evident across twelve consistent months without my monitoring for it.
